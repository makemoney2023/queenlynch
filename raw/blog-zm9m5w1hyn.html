Breathe Easy This Fall: Tackling Autumn Allergies with Queen Lynch Pharmacy.

As crisp leaves start to fall and sweater weather sets in, many of us are breathing a sigh of relief, only to find another sneaky worry hanging in the air: Autumn allergies. I am Lena from Queen Lynch Pharmacy, and you're not alone if you are among the many people in Brampton who are suddenly dealing with sniffles, sneezes, and itchy eyes. Let’s face it, fall is beautiful, but those airborne allergens? Not so much.

The Reasons Fall Allergies Are Severe

Although the pollen high of spring may not be present in autumn, some obstinate reactions can be triggered by ragweed, mould from fallen leaves, and even dust stirred up by seasonal tasks. Yes, fall allergies may be the cause of your coughing after doing garden work, waking up with congestion or experiencing an exacerbation of your asthma.

Identifying the Symptoms

Here are what to watch for:

  • Persistent sneezing or sniffing, especially outdoors or after leaf-raking.
  • Red, irritated eyes or sinus pressure that won’t quit.
  • Sneaky asthma flare-ups or tightness when you least expect it.
  • That nagging scratchy throat, even when a cold seems unlikely.

How Can Queen Lynch Pharmacy Help?

At Queen Lynch, we have made it our mission to help you enjoy autumn without sneezing. Here is how we support you:

  • Minor Ailments Clinic: Get quick, same-day relief prescriptions for symptoms like allergic rhinitis without having to see a doctor.
  • Expert Advice on Over-the-Counter Solutions: From antihistamines to nasal sprays and eye drops, we'll help you find the combinations that work best for your routine.
  • Vaccines and Flu Shots: Are you unsure whether your symptoms are due to allergies or the flu? A flu shot can help you distinguish and protect you, just in case.
  • MedsCheck or Personalized Consults: We meet one-on-one to review your medications, looking for interactions, optimal timing, and strategies to reduce seasonal flare-ups.

Practical Tips for Allergic Relief at Home

  • On days when allergies are high, keep the windows closed.
  • Rinse off outside pollen; a quick change is beneficial for both pets and clothing.
  • Hydrate and steam your face to clear blocked sinuses.
  • Layer up when going out; cool mornings and warm afternoons can be a real one-two punch for your body.
